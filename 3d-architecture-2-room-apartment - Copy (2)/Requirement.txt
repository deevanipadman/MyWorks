COSC3306 Final Project
Overview:
Create an interactive 3D architecture environment using Three.js that demonstrates your
understanding of 3D modeling, scene composition, lighting, materials, animation, and
camera control.
Requirements
1. Structure
• Build a complete architecture with at least 2 distinct rooms
• Include fundamental elements:
o Walls with appropriate thickness
o Floor and ceiling
o Doorways or openings connecting rooms
• Demonstrate proper use of geometric primitives and/or custom geometries
2. Room Details and Furnishing
• Each room must contain:
- Minimum 3-5 furniture pieces or objects appropriate to the room type
- Proper scaling and positioning of objects
- At least 3 diOerent materials with varying properties
3. Camera Navigation
Implement smooth camera controls allowing users to:
• Navigate through all rooms in the home
• Use OrbitControls or implement custom camera movement
• Smooth transitions between camera positions (bonus)
• Collision detection to prevent camera from passing through walls (bonus)
4. Animation
Animated objects (rotating fan, swinging door) in the rooms as needed.
